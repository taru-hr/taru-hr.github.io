About this template

A clean, one-page employment contract with the sections a Finnish contract typically needs — parties, job title, start date, employment type, probation, working hours, salary, annual leave, place of work, collective agreement (TES), confidentiality, termination notice and signatures. Fields to complete are shown in grey.

This is an original portfolio example for demonstration only. It is not based on or copied from any employer's confidential documents and should not be used as legal advice.
